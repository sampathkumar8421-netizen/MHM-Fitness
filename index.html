01 — Standard

Most gyms sell access. We sell a standard.

Come in, put the numbers up, leave the guesswork at the door. Nothing on this floor is here to entertain you — a platform, a bar, and a coach who knows your last tested lift by heart.

02 — Progression

Load, logged.

  1. 01

    Calibrated

    Every working weight comes off your last tested five-rep max — not your mood, not the mirror, not the rack neighbour.

  2. 02

    Recorded

    Sets, reps, RPE and tempo written down in-session. The logbook is the programme's memory, and it never forgets a bad week.

  3. 03

    Retested

    At week twelve the four lifts go back on the bar under a coach's eye, and the next block is written around what they actually did.

Ring = one training block. Twelve weeks, one full rotation.

03 — Method

Four rules, in order.

No periodisation spreadsheet you'll never open. Four rules, applied for long enough to matter.

  1. 01

    Progressive overload

    Each session, load or reps move forward. If neither can, we change the lift or the tempo — never the standard.

  2. 02

    Barbell first

    Squat, bench, deadlift, press. Everything else on the floor exists to support those four.

  3. 03

    Tempo before volume

    Three seconds down until it is automatic. Control is the difference between training and collecting fatigue.

  4. 04

    Recover on purpose

    Sleep, protein, one true rest day. Non-negotiable, and written into the block alongside the lifts.

Cable at 3 × 12 — tension builds until the stack clears the floor.
